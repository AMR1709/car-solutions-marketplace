# Product Vision

## Problem

Automotive buyers can struggle to determine whether a part fits their
vehicle, whether a seller is trustworthy and what happens if the
received product is incorrect.

## Proposed solution

Car Solutions will connect buyers with verified automotive sellers and
provide vehicle compatibility, transparent product information,
protected ordering and structured returns.

## Initial market

- Country: Pakistan
- Currency: PKR
- Language: English first, multilingual-ready
- Initial products: Parts and accessories
- Initial sellers: Invited and verified sellers

## Not included in the first release

- Complete car sales
- Auctions
- Financing
- Native mobile applications
- International marketplace
- AI recommendations

## Initial success condition

A buyer can select a vehicle, find a compatible product, order it,
track it and request support if it is incorrect.
